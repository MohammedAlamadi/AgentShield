# Scoring-quality audit: process note (stored with the merged results)

- **Labellers:** both review sets (reviewer_1 and reviewer_2) were prepared **with ChatGPT assistance** and then reviewed and saved by the team.
- **Pairs:** each item's two labels come from two different team members, in five rotating pairs.
- **Agreement:** 40/40, Cohen's κ = 1.00, recorded in `scoring_quality_audit_stats.json`. This **cannot be interpreted as independent human inter-rater reliability**.
- **Use of the labels:** human-audited labels are used only to estimate the error of the frozen automatic verdicts. They never change those verdicts or any headline metric (plan v1.0).
